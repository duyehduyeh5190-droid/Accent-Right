## 📖 Overview

OrthoepicTrainer is an immersive linguistic training environment designed to sharpen your command of Russian word stress (ударение). Where a dictionary gives you a single answer, OrthoepicTrainer gives you a **muscle for language** — the instinct to place the accent correctly without hesitation, in conversation, in presentation, in poetry.

Russian stress is notoriously mobile, unpredictable, and riddled with exceptions. Native speakers stumble over it; learners dread it. This trainer turns that chaos into a **structured, progressive, and genuinely enjoyable discipline**. Think of it not as flashcards, but as a personal vocal coach for the written word.

Whether you are a journalist preparing a broadcast, a student bracing for the ЕГЭ, a polyglot refining your ear, or simply someone who refuses to mispronounce "звони́т" ever again — this is your practice hall.

> **2026 Edition** — rebuilt from the ground up for speed, accessibility, and depth.

## ✨ Why OrthoepicTrainer Stands Apart

Most stress-training tools reduce a rich linguistic phenomenon to a binary correct/incorrect verdict. OrthoepicTrainer treats every word as a **living artifact** with history, variant acceptability, and contextual nuance.

- 🧠 **Adaptive difficulty** — the engine learns which syllable patterns trip you up and quietly reshapes the queue.
- 🎧 **Pronunciation playback** — hear canonical stress rendered by synthesized or recorded voices.
- 📚 **Curated corpora** — thousands of words organized by morphological family, frequency band, and exam relevance.
- 🕐 **Anytime, anywhere** — practice at 3 a.m. or during a commute; the tool never sleeps.
- 🌍 **Multilingual interface** — guides and hints available in several languages for non-native learners.
- 🔒 **Privacy-respecting** — your progress stays on your device unless you explicitly sync.

## 🚀 Core Feature Set

### 🎯 Stress Drills
Interactive exercises where you select the stressed syllable of a displayed word. Immediate feedback, explanations, and etymological breadcrumbs help you *understand* rather than merely *memorize*.

### 🔁 Spaced Repetition Engine
A modified SM-2 scheduler resurfacing words at the precise moment your memory is about to fade. This is the science of remembering, distilled into a tap.

### 📈 Progress Analytics
Visual dashboards chart accuracy over time, per-pattern strength, and streaks. Watch your curve climb from clumsy to confident.

### 🗂️ Thematic Word Packs
- Professions and titles (догово́р, жалюзи́, катало́г)
- Verbs with shifting stress (звони́ть → звони́т)
- Loanwords and their Russian accents
- ЕГЭ-specific bundles refreshed each academic cycle

### 🎮 Gamified Modes
Timed challenges, survival rounds, and daily quests turn repetition into play. Leaderboards celebrate consistency, not just raw talent.

### 🧩 Custom Lists
Import your own vocabulary — a lecture, a novel chapter, a work glossary — and let the trainer weave it into your routine.

### 🗣️ Voice Comparison
Record your pronunciation and overlay it against the reference model to hear the divergence instantly.

### 📱 Responsive Interface
From ultrawide monitors to pocket screens, the layout breathes and reflows without losing a single feature.

### 🌐 Multilingual Support
Interface strings and explanatory notes localized for a global audience of Russian-language enthusiasts.

### 🛎️ Round-the-Clock Assistance
A support channel staffed around the clock — because language anxiety doesn't keep office hours, and neither do we.
